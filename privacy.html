Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This policy covers this website (ciock.github.io/rolify-landing-page), run by Luca Oropallo. The Rolify app has its own privacy details, which you can find on its App Store and Google Play pages.

What we collect

This website has no accounts, forms or cookies of its own. We use the Reddit Pixel to measure how well our ads on Reddit work. When it runs, Reddit may collect:

  • that you visited this page, and when;
  • whether you tapped the App Store or Google Play button;
  • technical data such as your IP address, browser, device and the Reddit ad you came from;
  • cookies or similar identifiers that let Reddit connect the visit to an ad click.

We only see totals in Reddit's ad reports. We never see your name or your Reddit account. Reddit handles this data as described in the Reddit Privacy Policy.

Your choice

If you are in Europe (worked out from your browser's time zone), the Reddit Pixel only loads after you tap Accept in the consent bar. If you tap Decline, it never loads. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. To change it, clear this site's data in your browser and reload the page.

You can also turn off personalised ads in your Reddit account settings, or block tracking with your browser's privacy features.

We read the utm_source and utm_campaign values in the page address and pass them on to the App Store and Google Play. That way we can tell which ad led to a download. These values describe the ad, not you.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. For data collected by the Reddit Pixel, contact Reddit. For anything else, email us.
